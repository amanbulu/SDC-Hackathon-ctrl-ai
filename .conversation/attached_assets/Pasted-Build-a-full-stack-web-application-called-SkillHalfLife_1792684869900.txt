SKILL HALF-LIFE
Implement the formula:

relevance = initialRelevance \* 2^(-elapsedTime / halfLife)

Use consistent time units and validated inputs. Include illustrative skills such as programming, cloud computing, cybersecurity, system design, AI literacy, and communication.

Allow switching between Moderate AI Disruption and Fast AI Disruption. Use different clearly documented scenario assumptions.

Show interactive skill relevance charts. Label all initial scores, half-lives, and future projections as illustrative model assumptions, not verified labour-market forecasts.

Distinguish skill relevance decay from task automation exposure. Do not imply that a skill's decay score predicts when a worker will lose their job.

Create a transparent, deterministic matching formula based on:

- Existing skill alignment
- Career interests
- Relevant experience or education
